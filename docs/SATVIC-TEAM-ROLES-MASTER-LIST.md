# Satvic Movement Team: Master List of Roles & Responsibilities

**Source:** https://satvicteam.lovable.app/ (home page + all 11 role pages, including collapsed FAQ answers)
**Captured:** 25 October 2026
**Purpose:** A reference for ISKCON / Hare Krishna Movement Hyderabad on how a values-driven, media-first Indian organisation structures its team, writes its role descriptions, and runs hiring. Part 3 maps what is reusable for our own projects.

### Four core values

1. **Service Before The Self.** Decisions are made on impact, not money, fame or benefit.
2. **A Spirit Of Joy.** Celebrate small achievements and milestones.
3. **Under Promising, But Over Delivering.** Do more than expected in every design, video or event.
4. **Simplicity In Delivery.** Communicate so clearly that "even a five-year-old can grasp it."

### "12 things that make working here different" (culture list)

1. You work to change how a country eats, lives and heals, not to grow profit.
2. What you create reaches millions of homes across India.
3. Colleagues become friends for life through shared work and shared values.
4. Plant-based lunch every day at HQ, dinner for whoever is still around.
5. Microgreens grown in-house, build-your-own salad bowl.
6. Protected quiet zones for deep work.
7. A camera is almost always rolling at HQ.
8. Team kirtan every few weeks "to slow down and give thanks together."
9. Fully sponsored courses from the best in each field.
10. Radical openness: pay and reasons for departures are discussed openly.
11. Beautiful workplaces (Bangalore HQ, Goa office).
12. Words on the wall as a daily reminder that "a small devoted group can change millions of lives."

### Hiring practices visible on the site

- Every role page uses the same template: one-line hook → Type / Location / Experience / Reporting-to → About the role → "The scale you'll be part of" → **What you'll own** → (sample work with view counts) → **Only apply if…** → **Don't apply if…** → What we offer → A note from the founders → FAQ → culture list → testimonial → apply.
- "The application is long by design. We are only looking for people genuinely called to this work."
- Response SLA: strong candidates hear back within 1 to 2 weeks; "We do not believe in ghosting candidates"; promising people are kept on file.
- Compensation stance: "We are not trying to find the cheapest person. We are trying to find the right person."
- Several roles require that the applicant has attended a Satvic workshop or practises the lifestyle.
- Several roles require daily use of AI tools (Claude is named) with something to show for it.
- Relocation help is offered for in-person roles.

## Part 2A. The 11 open roles (full detail)

### 1. Health & Wellbeing Content Lead
*Creative · Full-time · HQ (in person) · 2+ years · Reports to Subah Saraf*

**Hook:** Run the entire process of making a video, from idea and scripting to overseeing post-production.

**What you'll own**
- Choose the next topics using the topic-selection process and your own judgement.
- Brainstorm long- and short-form ideas, write scripts, take founder feedback, revise, make shoot-ready.
- Be present on shoots and direct long- and short-form videos.
- Coordinate researchers, scriptwriters and thumbnail designers.
- Run the pipeline from idea to published video so nothing gets stuck.
- Own the quarterly publishing goal and take responsibility for delays.

**Only apply if:** scripts you wrote were published; 2–3+ years in YouTube/Instagram content; you have led writers/editors/designers on a fixed timeline; you can explain what a video's views and watch time taught you; you live a health-focused lifestyle; you can name 3 health/food videos from other channels and say why each worked.

**Don't apply if:** health and food don't excite you; you want the founder to choose topics; you want remote/hybrid.

**What we offer:** videos in millions of homes; direct ownership of the whole pipeline; creative energy at HQ; plant-based lunch and like-minded team.

**FAQ:** "Is this a team management role only?" → No. Much of the work is brainstorming, pitching, scriptwriting and managing shoots yourself.

### 2. Head of Content
*Creative · Full-time · HQ · 3+ years · Reports to Subah Saraf*

**Hook:** Own the entire video pipeline, from the next big idea to a video the founder is proud to publish.

Responsibilities, must-haves, exclusions and FAQ are identical to Role 1 (the page is a copy with a higher experience bar and a more senior title). Sample work cited: "What the British Didn't Want Indians to Eat" (2.8M), "I Took India's Favourite Beauty Products to Court" (2M), "5 Daily Habits Destroying Your Brain Health" (2.7M), "Never Do THIS While Eating Your Food" (46M).

### 3. Video Editor (Yoga Wing, short-form)
*Creative · Full-time · listed as HQ, but FAQ says fully remote · 2+ years · Reports to Post-Production Supervisor*

**Hook:** Turn raw footage into short videos that stop a scroll and pull someone toward a calmer life.

**What you'll own**
- Build short-form yoga videos from raw footage, start to finish.
- Craft a visual hook in the first second.
- Cut, pace and sequence so every short holds attention.
- Add captions, music and clean motion graphics that feel premium, never noisy.
- Ship two to three strong shorts a week.
- Bring your own taste, not just a template.

**Only apply if:** you have editing work to show; confident in any professional editor; sharp eye for pacing and rhythm; obsess over small details; learn fast, take feedback without ego; want to move people, not fill a feed.

**Don't apply if:** casually exploring; need constant direction; see it as "just an editing job."

**FAQ:** Mostly editing, footage is handed to you. Fully remote, with clear briefs, regular check-ins and honest feedback on every cut. No yoga knowledge needed. Sample shorts cited at 11M–18M views.

### 4. Founder's Office Generalist
*Founder's Office · Full-time · HQ · 1+ years · Reports to Subah Saraf*

**Hook:** Work closely with the founder, take on new problems across Satvic, and see them through.

**What you'll own**
- Whatever is in the founders' heads: half-formed ideas (a new workshop, a product, a broken system) turned into a plan, staffed, and run until live. "You will not wait for a brief."
- **The dubbing project (first six months):** take the existing pipeline and dub every video into Tamil, Telugu and Kannada using AI voice cloning → review → upload to YouTube. Knowing the languages is not required.
- Writing in the founders' voice: team messages, emailers, event copy, product descriptions, applicant replies, until a draft goes out with a one-word change.
- Answering unanswered questions: should Satvic open a centre in a second city, what a new product category costs, which of three ideas to do first. Research, then recommend.

**Only apply if:** you started or ran something outside work/studies with results to show; you taught yourself a skill or tool; you use AI tools like Claude weekly with something to show; you have practised the Satvic lifestyle.

**Don't apply if:** straight out of college or never worked in a real business/agency; want to stay in one function; only like starting things, not finishing them.

**Founders' note:** "We do not need you to know everything. We need you to have been tested before… and to still be hungry to learn."

### 5. Customer Experience Executive
*E-Commerce · Full-time · HQ (fully in person) · 1+ years · Reports to Harshvardhan Saraf*

**Hook:** Be the person our community turns to when they need help, answers, or someone who listens.

**What you'll own**
- Reply to community members on WhatsApp, email and Instagram DMs.
- Solve order, delivery and workshop issues quickly and correctly.
- Escalate complex issues to the right team and follow up until fixed.
- Spot recurring problems and share them with the team.

**Only apply if:** high-volume support experience (Amazon is given as an example); clear written and spoken English and Hindi; have worked to and met response-time targets; attended a Satvic workshop or are in the community.

**Don't apply if:** no support experience; want remote; want every day to be different (most of the day is repeat questions).

**What we offer:** a path into other roles; daily plant-based lunch; open conversation about pay and departures.

### 6. New Product Development (NPD) Specialist
*E-Commerce · Full-time · HQ · 2+ years · Reports to Harshvardhan Saraf*

**Hook:** Turn simple product ideas into real products, from the first brief to the first order.

**What you'll own**
- Take each product brief (e.g. a trail mix, a natural Bournvita alternative) from idea to first order.
- Find, shortlist and negotiate with manufacturers; push samples until they meet the quality bar.
- Research what exists, what is missing, and what price works.
- Work closely with design and tech.
- Run several product projects at the same time.

**Only apply if:** you have found vendors, got samples and negotiated pricing yourself; taken a product from brief to first order; ~2 years in sourcing, procurement or product development; use AI tools daily to research and compare. Food/FMCG/wellness background is a plus.

**Don't apply if:** you want a strategy-only role; you want to manage vendors after launch (the Head of Operations does that) or work on farm sourcing.

**What we offer:** real ownership of how each product looks, feels and costs; freedom to find better manufacturers, materials and packaging.

### 7. Scientific Advisor
*Subject Matter Expert · Part-time (full-time or freelance also possible) · Hybrid (home + HQ) or remote · 3+ years · Reports to Subah Saraf*

**Hook:** Be the person Satvic trusts to make sure every health claim we publish is scientifically sound.

**What you'll own**
- Final scientific sign-off on every health, food and nutrition video before it goes live.
- Join the content team at the idea stage and question the claim before the script is written.
- Read the actual study behind each claim and check its numbers, findings and advice.
- Investigate scientific criticism of Satvic content and say clearly if it is right.
- Build a reusable library of studies, claims and ingredients.

**Only apply if:** you can judge whether a paper is peer-reviewed and strong enough; you can explain what vitamin K does without looking it up; you have reviewed or fact-checked health content pre-publication; you have explained science to non-scientists.

**Don't apply if:** no formal degree in food science, nutrition, medicine or public health (experience does not substitute); you only want to check citations after the script is done; you have never told a senior person their claim was not backed by evidence.

**What we offer:** "The power to stop a video when the science does not hold up."

**Founders' note:** "We want one person whose whole role is to protect [trust], and who will stop us when the science does not hold up."

### 8. Head of Outreach and Collabs
*Subject Matter Expert · Part-time · Hybrid · 4+ years · Reports to Subah Saraf*

**Hook:** Build Satvic's presence beyond our own ecosystem through media, partnerships, creators, and meaningful relationships. "This is not traditional PR."

**What you'll own**
- Pitch Satvic stories to journalists and editors and get them published (newspapers, magazines, online).
- Find and win awards, fellowships, recognitions and lists.
- Get Satvic onto podcasts, events and panels where the community already is.
- Close collaborations with creators and experts, from first message to final video or event.
- Bring influential people into Satvic through conversations, dinners and gatherings.

**Only apply if:** you have closed creator/podcaster/brand/expert collaborations end to end; can show a partnership that grew reach or credibility, with numbers; can name 7 Indian podcasts and 10 publications Satvic should be in, and why, without searching; have written pitches that got replies.

**Don't apply if:** you have never got a feature published; your PR work was press releases and waiting; you only worked where the network was already built.

**FAQ:** No agency background needed, but results required. No contact list is handed over: "You build the network from ground up."

### 9. Associate Product Manager
*Product & Tech · Full-time · HQ (flexible arrangement mentioned) · 1+ years · Reports to Harshvardhan Saraf*

**Hook:** Help turn ideas into products, pages and systems that millions of people can use. No coding required.

**What you'll own**
- Plan and launch new features for the Satvic app and website.
- Manage landing pages and web-page updates from brief to launch.
- Support the backend tools and flows that make workshops run.
- Write briefs for designers and developers, test what they build, and follow every issue until fixed.

**Only apply if:** 1–2 years in product, operations or project management; you have written a PRD that someone built from; you have tested a product, logged issues and followed them to closure; you use AI tools like Claude daily with something to show; you are part of the Satvic community or attended a workshop.

**Don't apply if:** you want every step defined for you; uncomfortable working across design/dev/other teams; not curious about technology; only want to plan and discuss, not test and launch.

### 10. Satvic Centre: Project Operations Lead (founding role)
*Founders and Builders · Full-time · HQ (in person; a week or two of WFH every few months allowed) · 5+ years · Reports to Subah and Harsh*

**Hook:** Find the location, build the team, open the doors, and run the first Satvic Centre in India from day one. The centre has five zones: cafe, wellness clinic, shop, multipurpose hall (yoga, kirtan, cooking every week), and community space.

**What you'll own**
- Lead the Bangalore location search, visit spaces, negotiate, close the deal.
- Brief the architect and interior designer; make every corner feel right.
- Map every licence and compliance requirement and track to final approval.
- Build and onboard the full team for cafe, clinic, shop and hall before opening (FAQ adds: the cafe team, a naturopathic doctor, a hall coordinator).
- Create the systems that keep all five zones running every day after launch.
- Build membership and community excitement before opening; make opening day unforgettable.

**First 90 days (FAQ):** get deep into the vision, lead the location search, brief the architect, research with the community what they actually want, map every licence and compliance requirement.

**After opening (FAQ):** you run it. Oversee all five zones day to day, maintain quality and culture, report to the founders.

**Only apply if:** you have opened or built a real physical space (cafe, boutique hotel, fitness studio, conscious F&B brand); you think in timelines and risks; you act without being told; no manual excites you; the centre vision excites you.

**Don't apply if:** you want to work from home; need daily direction; want a low-pressure job with switch-off times; don't resonate with the lifestyle; have never opened a space; are a coordinator rather than a builder.

**What we offer:** building a landmark for conscious living; site visits to the best wellness spaces in India, training; direct access to founders; daily Satvic meals.

**Autonomy (FAQ):** "Subah and Harsh set the vision and the standards. Everything else is yours to figure out and execute."

### 11. Accounts Executive
*Finance & Accounts · Full-time · HQ · 3+ years · Reports to Harshvardhan Saraf*

**Hook:** Own the numbers behind Satvic Movement, and make sure every rupee is tracked with absolute clarity.

**What you'll own**
- Daily bookkeeping, journal entries, accounts payable and receivable.
- Reconcile bank accounts, sales, returns, marketplace fees and payment gateways (Razorpay, Stripe, Shopify, Amazon).
- Make sure revenue from website, books, workshops and other sources reaches the accounts and matches the records.
- Calculate and record GST and TDS correctly (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, ITC matching).
- Keep books clean so the external CA can audit and file smoothly.
- Monthly MIS reports and basic financial statements (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, trial balance).
- Track product-level contribution margins (COGS, advertising, shipping, returns).
- Process salaries, vendor payments, farmer payments and other payouts on time.
- Maintain ledgers in Zoho or Tally.

**Only apply if:** 2–3 years of real accounting; understand debits/credits, journals, AR/AP; know GST, TDS and Indian compliance basics; can prepare and explain statements; comfortable reconciling banks, gateways and marketplaces; highly accurate; own deadlines.

**Don't apply if:** you only do data entry; you tolerate unexplained differences; you need chasing on deadlines; you find reconciliation tedious.

### 3.2 Practices worth adopting (beyond individual roles)

1. **A public "Join the team" page in this exact format.** One line hook, "What you'll own", "Only apply if", "Don't apply if", "What we offer", a founders' note, and an honest FAQ. The "Don't apply if" list is the single most effective element: it filters respectfully and saves interviewing time. We can use it for both paid staff and volunteer seva roles.
2. **Values stated as decision rules, not slogans.** "Service Before The Self" is already our language; their version is phrased as a decision rule ("we never decide based on money, fame or benefit, but impact"). Reword our values the same way.
3. **Write the culture list honestly.** Their "12 things" include prasadam-style shared meals, kirtan every few weeks, quiet zones, sponsored learning, and openness about pay and departures. Most of these are already true of temple life; saying so publicly is an asset for recruiting.
4. **Commitment to the lifestyle as an entry condition.** Many roles require having attended a workshop. Our equivalent: attended a yatra, a Gita course, or a Sunday programme. This keeps mission alignment high.
5. **AI tool fluency as a requirement, with proof.** "You use AI tools like Claude every day and can show something you built." Apply to content, founder's-office and product roles.
6. **Candidate respect SLA.** Reply within 1 to 2 weeks, never ghost, keep good people on file. Cheap to do, and it reflects Vaishnava etiquette.
7. **Reporting lines on every role.** Every page names who the role reports to. We should do the same for every seva role so accountability is clear.
8. **Quotas and ownership of outcomes.** Quarterly publishing goals, 2–3 shorts a week, response-time targets, first-order deadlines. Measurable seva is easier to support and recognise.
9. **A governance role for truth.** The Scientific Advisor is the model for a Shastric Review role across all channels. One named person, joins at idea stage, has veto, maintains a reference library.
10. **Programme "wings" with co-leads.** Youth, Yoga, Food each have their own lead, content and community people. Our departments (Yatra, Youth, Food for Life, Gita courses) can be organised the same way, each with a content person embedded.
11. **Regional-language dubbing as a defined project.** A six-month, pipeline-driven project with AI voice cloning and devotee review. Directly reusable for lecture archives.
12. **Build-and-run project template.** The Satvic Centre role is a reusable scope for any physical project: 90-day plan, licence map, team build, pre-opening community, then day-to-day ownership.

### 3.3 Suggested ISKCON role cards derived from this list

These are starting points, written in the Satvic format, for roles we do not currently define this clearly.

- **Shastric Review Lead** (part-time, senior devotee). Owns: sign-off on every lecture clip, reel, caption and book before publish; joins at idea stage; investigates criticism and answers it; maintains a verse and reference library. Only apply if: you can cite the source for a claim without searching, and you have told a senior devotee that something was not supported.
- **Yatra Experience Curator** (one per departure). Owns: the pilgrim's experience from registration to return; daily WhatsApp updates; issue escalation to the tour leader; post-yatra feedback and testimonials. Only apply if: you have been on at least two yatras and have handled a group under pressure.
- **Content Pipeline Lead** (full-time). Owns: topic selection, scripting, shoot direction, coordination of editors and designers, a quarterly publishing goal. Don't apply if: you want someone else to choose the topics.
- **Regional Dubbing Project Owner** (six-month project). Owns: AI dubs of the lecture archive into Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Hindi; devotee review rota; YouTube upload and metadata.
- **Community Care Responder** (WhatsApp/email/IG). Owns: replies within a set target, escalation, and a weekly list of recurring questions for the departments.
- **Temple Project Operations Lead** (build-and-run). Owns: site, architect brief, licences, team build, pre-opening membership, and daily operations after opening.
- **Accounts Executive with per-yatra margins.** Owns: gateway reconciliation, 80G receipts, MIS, and contribution margin per yatra, festival and product, feeding the economics screens in this app.
