# HR Department - Roles & Responsibilities additions (October 2026)

Derived from recurring instructions in the HR WhatsApp chats. To be appended to the Roles & Responsibilities document of each person so these become permanent responsibilities.

## Gnaneshwar - HR Executive (permanent / recurring responsibilities)

1. **Daily Performance Report** - Every working day by 11 AM: previous day's Target vs Actual for all 41 employees (drivers via register), red flags for non-achievers, late logins, unplanned leaves. Weekly, bi-weekly and monthly roll-ups. Shared as Google Sheet link + email to MKCD, STDD, RP.
2. **Daily Report compliance** - Collect daily report emails with ClickUp / Tata Tele screenshots from every employee; mark LOP / 0.5-day penalty for misses after written warning; monthly non-compliance list with reasons and penalties.
3. **Daily attendance report** - By 2 PM: present / absent / on-duty / week-off with Google Sheet link (already in practice - continue).
4. **ClickUp governance** - Ensure every employee has a seat and logs 8.5 hours; coordinate with Hem Chand for users / training; weekly ClickUp + Tata Tele usage report.
5. **Employee Master Data & CTC sheet** - Single live Google Sheet: employee master (contacts, emergency contacts, address, documents link, trust, designation) and monthly / yearly CTC; update within 1 day of any join / exit / revision.
6. **Attendance & late-login control** - Monthly late-login / LOP analysis with payroll; exceptions only with MKCD email approval; biometric and remote-punch management; 1st-floor access list.
7. **Appraisal data compilation** - Chase and collect appraisal forms; compile monthly / half-yearly / annual performance data per employee for payroll and appraisal decisions.
8. **Festival & event staffing** - Participation sheets (Ratha Yatra, Janmashtami, Radhashtami), calling confirmations, same-day status updates, advance-funds and bills reconciliation with same-day invoices to Finance.
9. **Recruitment support** - Profile sourcing & screening, scheduling, Keka Hiring module data entry, candidate feedback emails in standard subject format.
10. **Office closing & assets** - Check both floors (lights, AC, fans) before leaving; ID cards; systems / equipment requirement list; TSF equipment returns.
11. **Weekly pending-task update** - Every Monday: update the HR Task Tracker Google Sheet and email MKCD the pending list with status and support required.
12. **Roster integrity & employee living documents** - Every person paid on any trust appears in the HR roster with department, designation, date of joining, trust and a linked employee living document; reconcile roster vs pay sheets every month; link docs from the Notion Leadership Hub.
13. **ClickUp hygiene across spaces** - Weekly: close or merge duplicate / obsolete HR tasks in all ClickUp spaces (MKCP Assistant, Arjun Prabhu, MOM lists) and keep one live HR follow-up list.

## Rajendra Prasad - HR Manager (permanent / recurring responsibilities)

1. **Performance Management System owner** - Written targets for every employee (with reporting authorities); monthly appraisal; half-yearly and annual (March-April) cycle; salary proportional to performance; selection and rollout of an appraisal software.
2. **Recruitment pipeline owner** - Tele Sales Manager, PPC expert, Digital Marketing Manager, CSR / BDE and other approved roles; mandatory 4-round process (HR -> STDD -> MHBD -> MKCD); inform MKCD & STDD before every offer letter; aptitude / psychometric tests; Keka Hiring module.
3. **Payroll & statutory** - Pay sheets of all trusts to MKCD by 4th; incentives / variable pay included; monthly attendance to each employee before payroll; PF / ESI challans; salary advances and recoveries; consultant invoices.
4. **Trust structure & onboarding** - Every employee / consultant on the correct trust as per MKCD's list; offer / appointment letters; RGI compliance; copies of letters to MKCD.
5. **Policies** - Tour & Travel / Yatra allowance, mobile recharge, mobile-phone-at-work, Sunday & holiday annual roster, leave approvals, office hours, escalation discipline, non-performer exit with STDD.
6. **Rewards & Recognition** - Extra Mile award on 15th of every month with data-backed review; quarterly awards; monthly team meeting with snacks prasadam.
7. **Training & development** - Induction for new joiners; sales / team-building programs with recording, feedback and certificates; implement learnings from HR symposiums.
8. **Reporting to management** - MoM after every management meeting; monthly HR dashboard (headcount, attrition, attendance, performance, recruitment status); weekly pending-task status with Gnaneshwar.
9. **Availability** - HR Manager or HR Executive present in office during the 1st week of every month (payroll week); no simultaneous leave.
10. **Written warnings before payroll** - Every non-performance / target-shortfall / unapproved-absence case gets a written warning email before the month's payroll, with the salary consequence stated; copies filed in the employee file and a list sent to MKCD.
11. **Exits, consultants & trust movements - due process** - Performance record, notice, final settlement and letters for every exit; written consultancy agreements (scope, fee, IP, days on site); appointment letters and PF paperwork for every trust movement.
12. **Organisation structure & JD document owner** - Maintain the department R&R documents and employee living documents: designation, JD, CTC and reporting line for every person (PID39-42); backup person and KT plan for every role (PID101); communicate structure changes to all staff.
13. **Policies & SOP library for HR** - Recruitment & onboarding SOP, promotion policy, office-hours / Sunday / discipline circular, employee-monitoring disclosure; quarterly training calendar (PID15/16).
