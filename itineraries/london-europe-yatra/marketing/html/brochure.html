Every meal is Satvik prasadam, and the group travels together in one private coach with an experienced tour manager.

Good to know

Weather in late April

London 7–15 °C with showers; Paris to Cologne 8–17 °C; the Black Forest close to 0 °C at night; Mount Titlis about −5 °C in snow; Italy 10–21 °C. Pack layers, a warm jacket, gloves and cap for Titlis, an umbrella and comfortable walking shoes.

Walking

City days involve 6–8 km of walking and some standing. The tour manager plans rests, and a slower group with an assistant for seniors.

Ekadasi

Kamada Ekadasi (17 April) and Varuthini Ekadasi (2 May) fall on flight days. Fruit meals are ordered on all flights, and a grain-free dinner is served in London on 17 April.

Do

  • Carry your passport, visa and insurance card on travel days; keep a photocopy separately.
  • Be at the coach 5 minutes before the departure time announced.
  • Wear comfortable walking shoes already broken in; 10,000–15,000 steps on city days.
  • Dress modestly for churches and the Vatican: shoulders and knees covered, no caps inside.
  • Keep a small day-bag: water, cap, sunscreen, umbrella, light jacket, medicines, chargers.
  • Pre-inform the tour manager of medical conditions and dietary needs.

Don't

  • Don't carry valuables or large amounts of cash in back pockets or open bags.
  • Don't sign petitions or accept 'free' bracelets or roses from strangers (common scams).
  • Don't wander off alone; never leave the group without telling the tour manager.
  • Don't bring banned food items from India (meat, dairy, fresh fruit and vegetables into the UK/EU). Packaged food is mostly fine; declare it if in doubt.
  • Don't climb the Leaning Tower or do the Titlis Cliff Walk if you have heart, BP or knee problems.
  • Don't take photos in the Sistine Chapel or where signs prohibit it.
