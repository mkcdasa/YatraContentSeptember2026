# Content rules for every yatra

These rules come from the Hare Krishna Golden Temple yatra team. They apply to every yatra unless the team says otherwise for a specific one.

## Pricing and payment wording
- Show the price as: **₹50,000 + 5% GST · per person · excluding flight charges**, with the actual amount. Confirm "per person" with the team if not stated.
- Yatras are **all-inclusive**. Never write who pays for a meal or an activity. Banned: "group pays", "pay at counter", "complimentary", "MAP plan", "paid on actuals", "on your own", "optional (book online)".
- "Not included" normally lists only: flight charges, personal offerings/shopping/tips/porterage, anything not listed, GST 5%.
- Things the organisers book (ropeways, gondolas, battery cars, pony rides, passes) go under inclusions, never under exclusions.

## Daily rhythm (defaults)
- Breakfast **07:30–08:30**. Leave the hotel at **09:00** every day.
- Day 1 starts from the agreed arrival time (e.g. "arrive by 2 pm"). No Day 1 breakfast.
- Lunch at a pure-veg restaurant **close to the temple being visited**. Name a probable restaurant and a backup, each with a Google Maps link. Say these are probable choices that may change on the day.
- Dinner normally at the hotel.
- Keep evenings for kirtan, rest and briefings. Seniors travel, so avoid rushing.

## Food
- Pure vegetarian throughout; "no onion-garlic food on request".
- Check the panchang for **Ekadashi** dates inside the yatra. Plan grain-free prasadam that day and note the parana (fast-breaking) window next morning. Do not write "menu pre-ordered with the hotel".

## Places and maps
- **Every** place visited and every restaurant gets a Google Maps link: `https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=<name, area, city>`. Driving legs get directions links (`/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=…&destination=…`).
- Only list places the team has confirmed. When the team removes a place, remove it everywhere: brochure, poster, itinerary, docs, Excel, SQL.
- Don't promise what you can't guarantee. Wording like "arranged once for the group, as allotted by the Shrine Board" is honest. "Both Aartis guaranteed" is not.

## Highlights and USP
- Put the yatra's strongest hook in the **kicker badge** on cover and poster (e.g. "Helicopter Yatra · Aarti at the Holy Cave").
- Poster highlights: exactly 8 short lines, each under about 40 characters.
- Always mention the spiritual programme: kirtans, bhajans, pastimes, sthala purana, interactive sessions, managed by temple devotees.

## Dates and booking
- Every batch shows: dates, duration (e.g. "3 Nights / 4 Days"), **registrations close** date, and flight guidance (arrive by / depart after).
- Default timeline the team used: registrations close about 78 days before departure, yatri data to the travel agent 5 days later, tickets booked 2 days after that. Confirm for each yatra.
- When choosing dates, avoid peak-crowd festivals at that shrine (e.g. Navratri for Devi temples) and bad-weather seasons. Start batches on a Saturday unless told otherwise.

## The yatri itinerary (for circulation to registered yatris)
- **No timings at all.** Only places, what we do there, and the overnight stop.
- Opening section: "A spiritually enriching yatra" with the six promise cards.
- Meal plan on its own page: Day × Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner, with restaurant names and map links.
- End with the help-line phone number and the closing blessing.

## Photos
- Use photos the team supplies first. Otherwise use Wikimedia Commons images with clear licences (CC BY / CC BY-SA / public domain). Record every credit in `imgmap.json`; the brochure back page prints them automatically.
- Use circles for the 3 cover/poster photos and rounded boxes elsewhere.

## Translations
- Telugu (te), Hindi (hi), Kannada (kn) and Tamil (ta) are supported. Keep phone, websites, "Punya Kshetras", "ISKCON", GST, numbers and photo credits in English.
- Restaurant and place names are transliterated, not translated.
- Ask a native speaker to proofread before printing.

## Before you call anything finished
- Run the overflow check (the build does this) and look at the cover, the price page and the poster.
- Search the text for banned words (see Pricing) and for any place the team removed.
